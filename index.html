SpinBrawl privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

SpinBrawl is an arcade game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Everything the game remembers stays on your phone. The only time anything leaves it is when you play with friends online: then your player name, your spinner and your moves go to the other players in your room, through our room server, and nothing is kept once the room closes. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What the game stores on your phone

To keep your progress, the game saves these on your device only:

  • the player name you choose (it is not checked against any server),
  • your levels, ranks, coins, quests, chests, skins, icons and maps,
  • your career stats and settings, such as sound on or off.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes all of it. Android's own backup may copy app data to your Google account if you turned backup on; that backup is run by Android and Google, not by SpinBrawl.

Play with friends (online)

If you create or join a room with a room code, the game connects to SpinBrawl's room server, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc. acts as our service provider). While you are in a room:

  • the other players in the room see your player name, your spinner, its skin and your profile icon;
  • during a match, your controls (the stick and the buttons you press) go to the phone of the player who created the room, which runs the match and sends everyone what happens on the arena;
  • the room server only passes these messages between the players in the room. It keeps your name and spinner only for as long as you are in the room and deletes them when you leave or the room closes. Nothing is kept for later, and nothing is used for ads or profiles.

To connect you, Cloudflare's network necessarily handles your device's IP address, as any website or online service does. SpinBrawl does not log or store it. The connection is encrypted (secure WebSocket, TLS).

You don't have to use online play: the rest of the game works fully offline. Rooms are only for people who share the code with each other; there is no public chat.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No purchases with real money.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • No network requests outside Play with friends: the game, its art and its fonts are all inside the app. The Normal and Ranked lobbies are played against AI opponents on your phone.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the UK, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Because SpinBrawl keeps nothing about you on a server once your room closes, there is usually nothing to access or delete; your progress on your phone is deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Online play uses your data only to run the match you chose to join (the legal basis is providing the service you asked for). You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

SpinBrawl is not directed at children under 13 and keeps no data about anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, ads or purchases, or changes what online play sends, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
